Haushaltsankauf24 — Rebuilding a household clearance business online

A household clearance company serving North Rhine-Westphalia was trading under a domain nobody could spell, on a site that asked visitors to work for the contact details. The rebuild moved the business to haushaltsankauf24.de and rebuilt the path from "I need this cleared" to "someone is coming to look at it".

01 Overview

A local service business, hard to find and harder to trust

Haushaltsauflösung — clearing out a household — is something people arrange at difficult moments: a death in the family, a move into care, a property that has to be emptied before it changes hands. The visitor is rarely shopping around for fun. They want to know that the company is real, what it costs, and how fast someone can come.

The original site, haushaltsaufloesungennrw.de, worked against all three. The domain was long and easy to mistype, which quietly costs a business every referral made by word of mouth. The new domain, haushaltsankauf24.de, is shorter, spellable over the phone, and says what the business does.

02 Diagnosis

What was in the way of an enquiry

✕ Challenges

  • A domain too long to say out loud or type correctly from memory
  • No immediate answer to "do you serve my city?" — decisive in a regional trade
  • Pricing and process left unexplained, so visitors had to call to learn anything
  • Trust signals absent on a purchase people make while under stress
  • Contact buried below the fold on mobile, where most local searches happen

✓ Design Direction

  • Lead with coverage: name the NRW cities served, above the fold
  • Make the process explicit — assessment, offer, clearance, handover — so nothing feels hidden
  • Put a phone number and enquiry form within reach from every section
  • Separate the two offers, clearance and purchase, instead of blurring them
  • Build it in Elementor so the client can edit copy without a developer
03 Before / After

The same business, a different first impression
